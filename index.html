McKinsey & Company (2025)

Innovation Olympics Semi-Finalist — AI Performance Support Agent

Designed and built an AI agent to deliver performance support at scale across McKinsey — reaching the semi-finals of the Firm's internal Innovation Olympics competition, which surfaces high-impact innovations from thousands of colleagues globally. As the primary author of the agent's AI instructions, I designed a data-driven, methodologically rigorous evaluation framework — validated by the People & Analytics (PAM) team — to assess the agent's reliability and accuracy. The project required navigating a high-ambiguity research environment: one collaborator noted, "Marco challenged us when we considered quitting the experiment early. This work runs in parallel with a proof-of-concept for an AI-based skills learning platform that enables deliberate practice through informal learning — tracking skill mastery via a Learner Record Store, integrating with McKinsey's broader learning ecosystem via APIs, and providing personalized feedback based on rubrics and user-generated data. The platform directly supports the Firm's Future of Learning strategy.

McKinsey & Company (2024–2025)

AI-Powered Learning Agents — Value Proposition Chatbot & Roleplay Simulations

Designed and built two AI-powered performance support tools for McKinsey professionals. The CCN Value Proposition Agent is a developmental chatbot that guides colleagues through the challenging process of articulating and refining their professional value proposition in the age of AI. After gathering requirements from CCN Directors and mapping the agent's user flow, I iterated on the design to balance open-ended development opportunities with structured guidance — a sensitivity inherent in helping professionals confront how AI is reshaping their roles. The agent is currently in champion testing globally.

Separately, I designed AI-powered voice-based roleplay simulations to give Partners a safe, realistic environment to practise critical client-facing skills — embedding deliberate practice directly into the flow of work using conversational AI. Both solutions leverage prompt engineering, agent training, and iterative evaluation to deliver learning that adapts to the learner.

McKinsey & Company (2024–2025)

EPR-Based Feedback Simulation — Executive Directors & Team Leaders

Designed and developed an assessment-level simulation using Attensi to help Executive Directors and team leaders globally navigate the complexities and emotional weight of giving developmental feedback. Unlike instructional simulations designed to teach, this project was built to evaluate competence — a design challenge requiring extended learning needs analysis, structured SME collaboration across global teams, and meticulous interaction storyboarding. A senior stakeholder noted: "Marco meticulously storyboarded every interaction with detailed flow charts that allowed our SME to see the nuances of the simulation and make informed suggestions during the process."

I applied learning analytics rigorously throughout piloting — using simulation data to calibrate the difficulty of each scenario and validate the experience iteratively before Firm-wide rollout to 1,000+ users. To push the boundaries of the Attensi platform, I developed custom interaction designs that one reviewer described as creating "an experience that immersed learners and allowed application-level assessment — something not previously attempted asynchronously."

McKinsey & Company (2024–2025)

AI & Data Literacy Upskilling — L&D Design Team

Following the completion of an Ed.D. in Learning Analytics, I designed and led a comprehensive upskilling initiative to build data fluency and AI competence across McKinsey's Learning Design team. Drawing on original dissertation research, interviews with the People & Analytics (PAM) team, and benchmarking of emerging practices in AI-augmented learning, I created a skills roadmap covering data-driven learning design, impact measurement, and AI integration in L&D workflows.

Delivery formats included: co-facilitating bi-weekly AI Office Hours; leading sessions on Learning Needs Analysis and Performance Objectives; facilitating cross-functional D2 Impact Measurement calls with PAM guests; co-creating an "AI Lightbulb Moments" knowledge base capturing team discoveries; and presenting "data collection beyond the LMS" to the wider Impact Measurement Community of Practice. Over nine months, team GenAI adoption increased from 60% to 95% weekly usage — a direct outcome of consistent, practitioner-led capability building embedded in everyday work.
